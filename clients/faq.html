Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about booking, cancellations, tours, and services.

How do I request a custom travel package?+

You can request a custom package by visiting our booking page and selecting your destination. Use the "Special Requests" box to add hotel upgrades, private tours, or custom details. You can also submit an inquiry on the Services page.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?+

Cancellations made more than 7 days before the travel date qualify for a full refund. Cancellations made 3-7 days prior qualify for a 50% refund. You can request changes or cancellation through the Service Center Forms.

Is transport included in the destinations packages?+

Yes, many of our local destinations (like Kirisur Farm and Honda country tours) include options for guided group transport or vehicle rentals. Review the details on the destination page or request transport in your booking form.

Do I need an account to book a trip?+

While you can submit an inquiry without an account, we recommend registering so you can view your booking status and manage your profile details directly from your dashboard.
