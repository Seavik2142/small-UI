Privacy Policy

Learn how we collect, store, and manage your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data to process your bookings, verify your identification, and improve our services. This includes:

  • Your contact details: Name, email address, phone number.
  • Identification details: Passport / ID numbers for hotel and transport check-ins.
  • Transactional data: Booking reference history, ratings, and payment logs.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely for the fulfillment of travel reservations. We share necessary identification details only with authorized partners (like Kirisur Farm, Honda Powersports, or local hotel hosts) for checking booking status.

3. Data Retention & Security

We store profile details and booking logs in your local storage or secure server environments. You have full control over your profile and can request to review or delete your personal account at any time through your dashboard.
