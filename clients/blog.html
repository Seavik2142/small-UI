When to go to Goa (Spoiler: Not Summer)

We got burned by monsoon season. Goa is beautiful, but visiting it during the wrong time of the year can completely ruin your plans. The summer months in Goa can be extremely hot and humid, followed by a heavy monsoon season that shuts down most beach shacks and activities. Discover the perfect window for white sand, refreshing breezes, and budget-friendly hotels.
