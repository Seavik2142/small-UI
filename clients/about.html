Our Story & Mission

Providing authentic travel and lifestyle experiences across Phnom Penh and Cambodia.

Who We Are

Founded in 2024, Free Travel was born out of a desire to make trip planning transparent and accessible. We grew tired of crowded tour buses, hidden fees, and standard itineraries. We set out to build a platform that focuses on custom travel packages, curated local recommendations, and exceptional guest support.

Whether it's shopping at Chip Mong, riding Honda dirtbikes through Cambodia's rugged landscape, or exploring the beautiful Kirisur Farm, we organize seamless bookings and custom events tailored to your needs.
